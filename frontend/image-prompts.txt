ZETTOS INTERIOR UNITS — IMAGE GENERATION PROMPTS

Art direction for every image
Premium architectural editorial photography; photorealistic; natural daylight;
rich but restrained materials; contemporary African design influence where
appropriate; thoughtful composition and realistic proportions; no text, logos,
watermarks or identifiable client details. Keep colours warm and sophisticated:
forest green, terracotta, muted gold, ivory, timber and charcoal. Images are
concept visualisations, not depictions of completed Zettos projects.

editorial interior architecture photography, 16:9.
