4. My recommended implementation strategy
Although the prompt contains the entire product vision, I would build this in stages rather than ask Copilot to generate everything in one uncontrolled pass.
1. Brand identity and homepage

   Establish the logo, typography, colour system, navigation, hero section and homepage experience first.
2. Services and portfolio

   Build the service pages, project categories, individual project pages and image galleries.
3. Backend and admin dashboard

   Implement authentication, database, project management, services, enquiries, uploads and content management.
4. Testing and launch

   Test all functionality, replace concept imagery with actual company projects, configure business details and deploy.
